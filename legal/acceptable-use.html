SeedCode CLI: Acceptable
Use Policy.

Last updated: 5 November 2026. This policy applies to everyone who uses SeedCode CLI.

1. Purpose

SeedCode CLI is a developer tool. It may only be used for lawful work that you are authorised to perform, in line with the Terms & Conditions and the PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0.

2. Prohibited activities

  • Breaking any applicable law, regulation or court order.
  • Attacking, scanning, disrupting or gaining unauthorised access to systems, networks or accounts.
  • Creating or distributing malware, ransomware, spyware or other harmful code.
  • Deleting, altering or exfiltrating data you do not own or have permission to change.
  • Using SeedCode CLI on a machine, repository or account without the owner's authorisation.
  • Circumventing payment, subscription, rate limits or access controls of any provider, including GitHub Copilot.
  • Generating content that sexually exploits minors, incites violence, or harasses or threatens people.
  • Infringing intellectual property or violating privacy or data-protection law.
  • Commercial use, unless you hold a separate commercial license from the author.

3. Abuse prevention

You must not attempt to overload, reverse engineer or bypass the security controls of the CLI, its providers or the systems it reaches. You must not resell, sublicense or redistribute provider credentials. Automated use must respect the limits imposed by the provider you use. Do not use the agent to run commands that the operating system, your organisation's policy or a provider's terms prohibit.

4. Service misuse

Do not misrepresent AI-generated output as vetted, audited or guaranteed work. Do not use SeedCode CLI to impersonate a person or organisation, to produce deceptive security claims, or to bypass a provider's content or safety policies.

5. Account and access restrictions

SeedCode CLI has no hosted SeedCode accounts to suspend in this release. The author may refuse support, remove contributed material, or terminate a license for material breach of these rules. Your access to GitHub Copilot, OpenRouter or any other provider can be limited or revoked by that provider under its own terms, independently of SeedCode.

6. Reporting abuse

Report misuse of the SeedCode name or project to github@eagox.studio. Report security vulnerabilities through the security page.

7. Changes

This policy may be updated as the project changes. The current version is always published here. Material changes will be noted in the project changelog.
